# 03 - HR policy

This file is the placeholder source of truth for rules used by the API rule engine and AI retrieval. Replace examples with approved company policy before using real employee data.

## Approval principles

- Hard rules are enforced by the API and cannot be overridden by the agent.
- The agent may explain a rule and identify missing information, but must not invent policy.
- Requests outside policy, ambiguous requests, and high-risk actions are escalated to a human.
- Record policy version and rule outcomes in the audit trail for every decision.

## Policy facts to confirm

- Leave types, accrual, carry-over and notice requirements.
- Work-hour limits, overtime and rest-period requirements.
- Attendance correction evidence and location/privacy requirements.
- Shift-change notice, staffing minimums and manager authority.
- Agent autonomy levels, prohibited actions and escalation thresholds.
